Cookie Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

This page explains what is stored on your device when you use CineStream, why, and how to control it. It supplements our privacy policy.

What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file a site stores in your browser so it can recognise the browser on a later request. Related technologies, including local storage and similar device identifiers, do much the same job. This policy covers all of them.

Advertising cookies in detail

CineStream is free and is funded by advertising. Google and its advertising partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other sites, to cap how often you see the same ad, and to measure whether an ad was effective.

  • Turn off personalised advertising for your Google account at Google Ads Settings.
  • Manage a broader list of advertising vendors at aboutads.info or, in Europe, youronlinechoices.com.
  • Where local law requires it, we ask for consent before personalised advertising cookies are set. Declining does not remove advertising, but the ads shown are non-personalised.

Controlling cookies in your browser

Every major browser lets you view, block and delete cookies from its settings, usually under Privacy. You can block third-party cookies specifically, which stops most advertising cookies while leaving sign-in working. You can also browse in a private window, which discards everything when you close it.

Blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent you from signing in, which in turn disables My List and Continue Watching. Everything else on the site, including the whole catalogue and every article, continues to work.

Do Not Track

There is no agreed standard for how sites should respond to a Do Not Track browser signal, and we do not currently respond to it. The opt-out links above are the reliable route.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

This policy explains what information CineStream collects, why we collect it, who else receives it, and what you can do about it. CineStream is operated by VAF Ubwenge Tech, Kigali, Rwanda.

1. Information you give us

You can browse the entire catalogue, read every article, and watch trailers and public domain films without an account. We only collect personal information if you choose to sign in.

Signing in uses Google Authentication. We never see or store your Google password. From that sign-in we receive and store your name, email address, profile photo URL and a Google account identifier. Once signed in we also store the titles you add to My List and your watch history, which records which titles you started and how far through each you were.

2. Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records standard server log information when a page is requested: IP address, browser and operating system, referring page, and the time of the request. This is used for security and to diagnose faults.

We store a small amount of information in your browser's local storage, such as your light or dark theme preference. That data stays on your device.

4. Advertising

CineStream displays advertising served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info or youronlinechoices.com.
  • Where required by law, visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised advertising cookies are set, and ads are served non-personalised if consent is declined.

Our cookie policy lists the categories of cookie in use and how to control each of them.

6. International transfers

Our providers operate infrastructure in several countries, so your information may be processed outside your own. Where data is transferred out of the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, our providers rely on the safeguards published in their own terms, including standard contractual clauses.

7. How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account exists. Watch history is capped at your twenty most recent titles and older entries are discarded automatically. Server logs are retained by our host for a short operational period. When you ask us to delete your account, we remove your stored profile, list and history.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to object to or restrict certain processing, to receive a portable copy, and to withdraw consent for advertising cookies at any time. To exercise any of these, email us from the address your account uses and we will respond within five business days. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

CineStream is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored with our providers under access rules that restrict each account's records to that account. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of the page. Material changes will be highlighted on the site.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy, or any request relating to your data, go to techubwenge@gmail.com.

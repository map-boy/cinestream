Disclaimer and Copyright Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

This page states plainly where our video comes from and what to do if you believe a title on CineStream infringes your copyright.

1. We do not host or distribute films

CineStream stores no video files. Nothing is uploaded to us, mirrored by us, or served from our infrastructure. Two external sources supply everything that plays on the site:

  • Trailers play through YouTube's official embedded player, directly from YouTube's servers under its standard embed terms.
  • Full-length films play through the Internet Archive's embedded player, and only for titles we have verified against that archive as public domain.

We do not use unlicensed third-party video hosts, aggregator embeds, or file lockers to expand the playable catalogue. Where a public domain copy does not exist for a title, we offer no playback at all rather than substituting another source.

2. Most of the catalogue is not playable in full

The browsing catalogue is far larger than the playable library, and that is by design. For any title still under copyright, CineStream shows metadata only: synopsis, cast and crew, genre, runtime, rating and reviews, plus an official trailer where one exists. That information is presented for reference and discussion. To watch such a film, use a service properly licensed to distribute it.

3. How public domain status is determined

We rely on the published holdings of the Internet Archive, a non-profit digital library, to identify titles available for free distribution. This is a reasonable basis but not an infallible one. Copyright status differs between countries, a restored or re-scored version of a public domain film may carry its own separate rights, and an archived item can occasionally be uploaded in error. If you believe a specific title has been marked playable incorrectly, tell us and we will act on it.

4. Copyright notices

If you own or represent the owner of a work you believe is being made available through CineStream without authorisation, send a notice to techubwenge@gmail.com with the subject line "Copyright Notice". Please include:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed.
  2. The exact URL or title on CineStream that you are notifying us about.
  3. Your name, address, telephone number and email address.
  4. A statement that you have a good faith belief that the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  5. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorised to act on their behalf.
  6. Your physical or electronic signature.

On receipt of a notice that identifies a specific title, we suspend playback for that title while we review it, rather than waiting until the review is complete. We aim to acknowledge every notice within two business days.

5. Counter-notices

If playback for a title was suspended and you believe that was a mistake, or that the work is in fact in the public domain, send a counter-notice to the same address including your contact details, identification of the title, a statement under penalty of perjury that you believe the material was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification, and your signature. We will review it and restore playback if the claim is substantiated.

6. Repeat infringement

CineStream does not accept user uploads, so there is no user-generated content to police. Any source that generates repeated valid notices is removed from our verification pipeline entirely.

7. General disclaimer

Information on CineStream, including catalogue metadata and our written articles, is provided for general information. Metadata is community-maintained and may contain errors; our articles cover film history, copyright concepts and technical topics in general terms and are not legal advice. If you need to determine the copyright status of a specific work for your own purposes, consult a qualified lawyer in your jurisdiction rather than relying on this site.

8. External links

Where we link to external sites, we do so for reference. We do not control them and are not responsible for their content, and a link is not an endorsement.

9. Trademarks

Film titles, posters, logos and other marks appearing on CineStream belong to their respective owners and are used here for identification and reference. CineStream is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or certified by any studio, distributor, TMDB, YouTube or the Internet Archive.
